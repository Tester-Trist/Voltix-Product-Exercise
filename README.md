# Voltix Data Drop — Q3 Product Review Prep

Hi team,

Ahead of the Q3 roadmap review, here's a data pull covering the last ~2 months across a handful of accounts. Pulling this together so we've got a decent evidence base to work from rather than going purely off gut feel or whoever spoke up loudest in the last leadership meeting (you know who you are 😉).

## What's in this drop

- **`support_tickets.csv`** — a sample of support tickets across our accounts, exported from the ticketing system. Includes the freeform agent notes field, so expect some shorthand and typos, that's just how the agents write.
- **`nps_verbatims.csv`** — NPS survey comments from the same period, with scores.
- **`sales_lost_deal_notes.csv`** — notes from the sales team on deals lost this quarter, where they captured a reason.
- **`feature_usage.csv`** — weekly active usage % by feature, pulled from product analytics, as of the export date.
- **`exec_slack_thread.txt`** — a thread from #leadership-product that's relevant context for the roadmap conversation, thought it was worth including as-is.

## Notes on the data

- Account names are anonymised/simplified versions of our actual accounts, but the patterns are real.
- This isn't a complete dataset - it's a representative sample chosen to be manageable to review ahead of the meeting, not the full ticket/NPS volume.
- Timeframe is roughly the last 8 weeks.

Let me know if anyone needs anything pulled differently before the review.
